Stanislav Glazov

Media artist, educator, course author Founder of Licht.Pfad Studio (Berlin)

For more than 20 years, Stanislav Glazov has worked in light and visual art where art and technology intersect — from pipeline TD at Trixter Film and Scanline VFX to audiovisual performances at Berlin Atonal, Mutek, and Ars Electronica. In 2013 he founded Licht.Pfad in Berlin.

  • Before 2010

    Killer Tank, Pilot TV, early animation productions at Klassika Studio; Trixter Film and Scanline VFX: Pipeline Technical Director, Character Animation, Cloth Simulation TD, R&D VFX.

  • After 2010

    Automated real-time infographic broadcast system for Kommersant TV. Architectural video mapping with SilaSveta, Stereotaktik, Invisible, and Raduga Design.

  • 2013

    Founded the generative design and educational studio Licht.Pfad in Berlin.

  • AV

    Collaborations with WhiteVoid. Audiovisual performances in partnership with Dasha Rush and Robert Lippok.

  • Since 2016

    Stage and theatre productions with Maxim Didenko, including custom real-time media servers for live shows.

    Berlin Atonal, Mutek Montréal, Ars Electronica Linz, Unsound Kraków, Raster-Noton · Berghain, Barbican London, Resonance Tokyo, Mira Barcelona, L.E.V. Festival, Fête des Lumières Lyon.

      faq1 DAT6 questions
      01How do the courses work?

      All courses are recorded and self-paced. On the 101–305 courses every lesson is open from day 1; on the flagship Hou2Touch programme (/course) a new lesson opens every week (or full access on request). You keep access for 730 days.

      02How can I pay from my country, and is VAT included?

      You can pay by card (Stripe), PayPal, or bank transfer. All prices include German VAT (19%). If your company is registered in another EU country with a valid EU VAT ID, contact us before paying for a reverse-charge invoice without VAT.

      03Can I pay in two parts?
