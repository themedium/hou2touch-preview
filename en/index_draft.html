09FAQnull_out COMP

Frequently asked questions

01 How do the courses work?

All courses are recorded and self-paced. On the 101–305 courses every lesson is open from day 1; on the flagship Hou2Touch programme (/course) a new lesson opens every week (or full access on request). You keep access for 730 days.

02 How can I pay from my country, and is VAT included?

You can pay by card (Stripe), PayPal, or bank transfer. All prices include German VAT (19%). If your company is registered in another EU country with a valid EU VAT ID, contact us before paying for a reverse-charge invoice without VAT.

03 Can I pay in two parts?

On the flagship Hou2Touch course (/course, €2,000), yes — you can pay in two parts with the remainder due within 90 days.

04 What software and hardware do I need?

A Mac or Windows computer able to run TouchDesigner (and Houdini for the 207 Terrain and flagship Hou2Touch courses). Free non-commercial licenses (TouchDesigner Non-Commercial and Houdini Apprentice) are sufficient for learning.

05 Do you issue a certificate or grade homework?

We don’t issue certificates, and assignments are for your own practice (not graded or reviewed). By the end of each course, your completed assignments form a portfolio of working real-time systems.

06 What if I don’t finish within 730 days?

Write to us at stanislav.glazov@gmail.com and we will extend your access.
