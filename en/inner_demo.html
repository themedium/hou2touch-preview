БЛОК 01 About the course (тексты m2 + m1-about + Course aims)
Вариант 1A · Цепочка нод сверху + двухколоночный Bento-сплит (7fr / 5fr)
/extreme101/about1overview & architecture

Extreme was my first TouchDesigner course, and the name is the goal: to take a beginner and, extremely fast, build a deep understanding of how a node-based environment works. It brings you into the procedural way of thinking, explains the contexts and gives you enough working practice to use TouchDesigner confidently at an intermediate level.

Part 01 · Lessons 1–5 · 2D, CHOPs & Audio

Interface & node architecture, CHOP data & parameter control, timer-driven movie player, TOP compositing, colour, feedback and sound-reactive visuals.

Part 02 · Lessons 6–8 · Sync, Synth & 3D

Procedural animation, recording, multi-machine synchronisation, building a modular audio synthesizer in CHOPs, and rendering a basic 3D scene.

node-based workflowpart 1 → part 2
/extreme101/aims1course aims
  1. 01To introduce the node-based way of working in TouchDesigner and the main operator contexts, so a beginner can read and build a project without confusion.
  2. 02To teach how control signals, sound and images connect: CHOPs, TOPs, compositing, generative textures and audio-reactive control.
  3. 03To give practical skills in timing, video playback, multi-screen output and basic real-time 3D rendering as a foundation for further work.

Вариант 5A · Двухколоночный сплит «The Challenge / What You Build After» вместо двух голых абзацев
01 · the starting point (m2 paragraph 1)

You make music, images or installations and want visuals that respond as they happen, but you don't know where to begin. TouchDesigner looks strange at first, and 3D geometry can stop a beginner early — this course leaves 3D until the end and starts with how the program works.

02 · the result after 8 lessons (m2 paragraph 2)

After it, you build interactive images controlled by the mouse or live input and show them on one or several screens. Your visuals react to a microphone or music track down to the kick drum, you have your own movie player, multi-machine sync, and a basic 3D scene.
