      faq1 DAT16 questions
      01Why pay for the course if there are free tutorials on YouTube?Why pay if there are free tutorials?

      YouTube tutorials are made to teach one specific technique, usually without a system or a methodology. We teach you to understand systematically how the logic of node architecture works, with a balance between a practical and an academic approach.

      02Can I pay in instalments?

      Yes, in two parts: €1,325 now and the rest within 60 days.

      03Where can I read the terms?

      The terms are in the terms of service. Questions — write to hello@hou2touch.com.

      04Which software versions are the lessons recorded in?

      Lessons are recorded in Houdini 19.5 and TouchDesigner 2023.

      05What about software licences?

      TouchDesigner Non-Commercial is enough; Redshift is an optional bonus.

      06What language are the lessons in?

      English. Software interfaces and operator names are English, as in Houdini and TouchDesigner themselves.

      07Are there assignments, and who checks them?Are assignments required?

      Almost every lesson ends with an assignment you complete on your own.
