01 About the courseconcept1 COMP

My lights, screens and lasers need to work as one show

01 · the starting point

A client brief still has to become a show the venue can carry, with safety limits, budget and documentation settled first. Then TouchDesigner, GrandMA, DMX and lasers have to run as one system and stay reliable through the show.

02 · what you build

After the course you can develop a concept from a brief, check it against venue limits, and prepare a rental-based estimate. You visualise the rig in Capture, build the stage in Depence, and produce plots and patch lists. You build pixel mapping in TouchDesigner, program GrandMA2, share one rig between them over Art-Net, drive the console from MIDI and sound, and send graphics to a laser over Ethernet.

/lights204/about1overview & architecture
TouchDesigner 204: Real Lights — Level 1: Pre-production

This course develops the preproduction capability to translate creative live-event briefs into feasible lighting and multimedia productions. Learners integrate venue, safety, technical, commercial, and operational constraints to design spatial and control systems, visualise design intent, and produce the documentation and coordinated workflows required for reliable delivery.

TouchDesigner 204: Real Lights — Level 2: Production

This course develops the ability to design and operate integrated real-time systems for lighting, pixel-mapping, moving heads, and lasers. Working across TouchDesigner and GrandMA over networked DMX, learners map visual and geometric data onto fixtures and lasers, program coordinated cue playback, drive the console from MIDI and sound, and keep a show's output reliable and correctly prioritised.

/lights204/aims1course aims
  1. 01To provide an understanding of how a live-event lighting and multimedia production moves from client brief to delivery: lighting and control technology, venue and safety limits, roles, budgets and supplier agreements.
  2. 02To develop practical skills in visualising lighting and screen concepts in Capture and Depence and in producing the plots, equipment reports, renders and patch lists that a production team works from.
  3. 03To provide an understanding of how DMX and Art-Net carry control data to lighting fixtures and lasers, how geometry and UV maps link a venue model to its LED screens, what a show-laser scanner can draw safely, and how TouchDesigner and a GrandMA2 console share control of one rig.
  4. 04To develop practical skills in building TouchDesigner control, pixel-mapping and show-control systems (MIDI and sound) for fixtures, moving heads and LED rigs, preparing scanner-safe laser graphics, and programming cue playback in GrandMA2.

outputs1what you will be able to do
  1. 01Check a lighting and stage design against a venue's safety limits, including structural and wind loads, haze and fire alarms, sound, heat, lasers and strobes, and decide what needs qualified approval.
  2. 02Visualise a lighting rig and media screens in Capture, from importing the venue model to testing fixtures and haze, then produce plots, equipment reports and concept renders for a production team.
  3. 03Control PARs and moving heads from TouchDesigner over Art-Net/DMX with smooth 16-bit movement, and aim moving heads at real-world positions using XYZ coordinates and stage markers.
  4. 04Build a pixel-mapping engine in TouchDesigner that turns fixture positions and video into RGBW channel data, packs it into DMX universes and sends it over Art-Net to a visualiser, then find and fix order and flip errors.
  5. 05Program GrandMA2 from patch to playback: patch conventional, pixel and moving fixtures, build groups and presets, and record cue sequences with tracking and shaped transitions.
  6. 06Share one rig between TouchDesigner and a GrandMA2 console over Art-Net, merge their signals by priority, and drive the console from MIDI and sound with fader, cue and beat triggers.

    09 FAQfaq1 DAT

    FAQ

    /lights204/faq110 questions
    01Why pay for the course if there are free tutorials on YouTube?

    A free tutorial is often enough for a single technique. This course follows one line through a show across 40 hours of recorded video: client brief, venue and safety limits, budget, visualisation in Capture and Depence, then TouchDesigner and a GrandMA2 console sharing control of one rig over Art-Net. You get the whole chain in one order, including the plots, equipment reports and patch lists a production team works from.

    02What is the difference between Level 1 and Level 2?

    Level 1 is pre-production: you develop a concept from a brief, check it against the venue, prepare an equipment estimate, plan power, signal and control, and visualise the design in Capture and Depence. Level 2 is production: you build control and pixel mapping in TouchDesigner, program GrandMA2 from patch to playback, drive the console from MIDI and sound, and prepare laser graphics.

    03Which software does the course work in?

    TouchDesigner, GrandMA2 onPC, Capture and Depence. Ableton Live appears in the show control part, where it drives the console with MIDI and beat triggers.

    04How far does the laser part go?

    You learn how a show-laser scanner draws geometry and which speeds, sizes and point orders can damage it. Then you prepare, colour and animate laser graphics in TouchDesigner and send them to a Capture preview and to a real laser over Ethernet.

    05Will this course qualify me to approve the safety of a rig?

    No. Level 1 goes through structural and wind loads, haze and fire alarms, sound, heat, lasers and strobes, and who approves them. You learn to check a design against these limits and decide what needs qualified approval. The approval itself stays with those qualified people.

    06How long will I have access to the lessons?

    730 days. If you need more time, write to us and we extend it.

    07I work on a Mac. Will the course work for me?

    Yes.

    08Is the free version of the software enough?

    TouchDesigner Non-Commercial is enough for the whole course, with the limits of the free licence.

    09Is there a discount on software licences?

    Students of the course get TouchDesigner Commercial for $400 instead of $600.

    10Do you issue a certificate?

    We don't issue certificates.
